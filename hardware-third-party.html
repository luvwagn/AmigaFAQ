Hardware: Third Party

Upgrades and add-ons made by companies other than Commodore.

What is third-party Amiga hardware?

Third-party hardware is anything made by companies other than Commodore: accelerators, memory expansions, hard disk controllers, graphics and sound cards, network cards, and video production equipment. Much of the Amiga's long life comes from this hardware.

Top

Which companies made classic Amiga upgrades?

  • GVP (Great Valley Products) - accelerators and hard disk controllers.
  • Phase 5 - the Blizzard and CyberStorm accelerators and CyberVision graphics cards.
  • Village Tronic - the Picasso graphics cards.
  • NewTek - the Video Toaster video production system for the A2000.

Top

Is new Amiga hardware still being made?

Yes. Companies and hobbyists still make accelerators, memory, network and USB adapters, video upgrades and replacement parts. Examples include Individual Computers, the open-source PiStorm project, Terrible Fire's open-source accelerators and the Apollo Team's FPGA-based Vampire boards.

Top

Where can I look up a particular expansion card?

The Big Book of Amiga Hardware and the Amiga Hardware Database both catalog thousands of expansion cards and peripherals, with photos and specifications.
