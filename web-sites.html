Web Sites

Useful Amiga web sites for software, games, hardware, emulation and community.

Where can I download Amiga software?

  • Aminet - the largest archive of freely distributable Amiga software, running since the early 1990s.
  • WHDLoad - the official home of WHDLoad, with installers for running floppy games and demos from a hard disk.

Top

Where can I look up Amiga games?

  • Hall of Light - a very detailed database of Amiga games, with screenshots, credits and scanned manuals.
  • Lemon Amiga - a popular game database with user reviews and ratings.
  • Amiga Magazine Rack - scans of classic Amiga magazines, cross-referenced with the Hall of Light.
  • AmigaLove - in-depth reviews of classic games, plus articles and a friendly forum.

Top

Where can I find demos and music?

  • Pouet - the main database of demoscene productions, including thousands of Amiga demos.
  • Amiga Music Preservation - an archive of Amiga music modules and the musicians who made them.

Top

Where can I look up Amiga hardware?

  • Big Book of Amiga Hardware - a huge reference of Amiga computers, expansion cards, accelerators and peripherals, with photos and details.
  • Amiga Hardware Database - specifications and photos of Amiga models and expansion cards, with install disks, benchmark results and search by manufacturer, chipset or autoconfig ID.
  • AmigaOS Documentation Wiki - developer and technical documentation for AmigaOS.

Top

Where can I get Amiga emulators?

  • WinUAE - the most accurate Amiga emulator, for Windows.
  • FS-UAE - an emulator for Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • Amiberry - an emulator designed for Linux devices such as the Raspberry Pi.
  • vAmiga - an accuracy-focused emulator for macOS.
  • Copperline - a newer, free and open-source emulator for Windows, macOS and Linux, focused on cycle-accurate hardware emulation.
  • Amiga Forever - a licensed package of Kickstart ROMs, Workbench and emulation software.

Top

Where can I talk with other Amiga users?

  • English Amiga Board - one of the largest and most active Amiga forums, covering hardware, software, games and emulation.
  • A1K - a large German-language Amiga community.
  • amiga-news.de - long-running Amiga news, in English and German.

Top

Where can I get the current AmigaOS?

  • Hyperion Entertainment - the developer of AmigaOS 3.2 for classic Amigas and AmigaOS 4 for PowerPC systems.

Top

Which web sites work on a real Amiga web browser?

Most modern sites are too heavy for classic Amiga browsers, and many need encryption they cannot handle. A few sites are built for old computers:

  • FrogFind - a search engine that turns web pages into simple text-only versions that vintage browsers can show.
  • 68k.news - a news site made for vintage computers and browsers.
  • AmigaFAQ - this site, which works over plain HTTP on classic Amiga browsers.
