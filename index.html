Questions

What is the Amiga?

The Amiga is a family of personal computers launched by Commodore in 1985, known for its custom chipset, advanced graphics and sound, and preemptive multitasking operating system.

Can I still use an Amiga today?

Yes. Original hardware is still collected and repaired, new accelerators and expansions are produced by the community, and emulators let you run Amiga software on modern computers.

Suggestions and corrections are welcome — open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
